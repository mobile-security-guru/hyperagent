# IT Director questions

Work through these in order. Each answer becomes one or more rows in the actions table.

1. **Billed against managed.** Match carrier invoice lines to MDM-managed devices. List lines billed with no managed device, and managed devices with no billed line. Count each group.
2. **New phones.** For each new-phone ticket in the last 90 days: was the old device wiped or retired, did the sign-in factor move, and who approved the change? Which record shows each step?
3. **Resets by phone.** For password or MFA resets requested by call or text: how was the caller verified, and where is that verification recorded for a given ticket?
4. **Number changes.** Who is authorized on the carrier business account to move a number, swap a SIM, or port out? Is a port-out lock or account PIN in place, and can you produce the record?
5. **Leftover lines.** Which lines belong to departed users or shared devices and are still active? Who owns each one today?
6. **Enrollment coverage.** Of the devices signing in to email and chat, how many are enrolled in MDM? Report counts only.
