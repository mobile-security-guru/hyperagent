# CISO questions

Work through these in order. Each answer becomes one or more rows in the actions table.

1. **Phone as the approving factor.** In the Conditional Access policies and authentication methods policy, which privileged or financial actions are approved on a phone (push with number match, passkey on a phone, SMS or voice code)? List each action and the factor it accepts.
2. **Device conditions on privileged access.** For each policy that covers privileged roles: does it require a compliant or managed device? Which exclusions exist, who approved each one, and where is that approval recorded?
3. **Lost phone recovery.** When someone loses the phone that holds their factor, what path registers a new one? How does the help desk verify the caller, and which record shows that verification happened for a given reset?
4. **Phone number as a factor.** Which accounts still accept SMS or voice codes? Who can move that number to a new SIM or another carrier, and is a port-out lock or carrier PIN recorded anywhere you can produce?
5. **New device registration.** Can a user add an authenticator on a new phone without a second person or a ticket? Is there a record linking each registration to a request?
6. **Recent incidents.** For each of the last three identity incidents: which step happened on or through a phone, which system recorded it, and what was UNKNOWN at the time of the incident.
7. **Departures.** When someone leaves, are their phone-based factors and registered devices removed on the same day? Which record proves it?
